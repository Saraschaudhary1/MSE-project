About Us

Who we are

Asha Kiran Foundation is a non-profit started by a group of teachers and students. We believe every child deserves a school, every family deserves healthcare and nobody should sleep hungry.

Our Mission

To support underprivileged communities through education, health camps and food programmes, with the help of donors and volunteers.

Our Vision

A society where everyone has equal access to learning, care and dignity.

Our Causes

The main areas where your support makes a difference.

📚

Education

Free evening classes, books and school kits for children.

🩺

Healthcare

Free health check-up camps and medicine distribution.

🍲

Food Drive

Nutritious meals for children and daily wage families.

🌱

Green Earth

Tree plantation and clean-up drives in local areas.

How We Start an Initiative

Every project follows the same simple path, from spotting a need to reporting back to our donors.

1

Find the Need

Our team visits villages and slums, talks to families and lists the most urgent problems.

2

Plan with the Community

We decide the goal, the budget and the people required together with local leaders.

3

Raise Funds & Volunteers

We invite donors and volunteers to join. Every rupee is tracked.

4

Launch & Report

We start the work on the ground, then share photos and results with everyone who helped.
